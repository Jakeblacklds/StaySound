## Design Context

### Users
StaySound is for people who listen to and organize their own music library on mobile. They need fast access to albums, artists, playlists, and playback controls without the interface getting in the way of listening. The product should feel trustworthy, calm, and personal rather than flashy.

The new web surfaces serve two narrow audiences. The public landing is for local buyers who already understand what they want and simply need enough trust and clarity to message directly. The private admin panel is for the owner operating the product manually, so it should feel fast, quiet, and businesslike rather than app-store promotional.

### Brand Personality
Grounded, musical, intentional.

The interface should feel curated and tactile, with enough warmth to feel personal but enough restraint to feel reliable. It should not drift into generic "AI app", glassy neon, or overly playful startup visuals.

Copy should be brief and adult. Avoid tutorialized language, filler reassurance, or text that explains obvious interactions.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the existing Material 3 and dynamic-color foundations, but keep surfaces denser, calmer, and more purposeful. Favor strong hierarchy, balanced spacing, and subtle contrast over decorative gradients or oversized rounded pills. Album art and content should carry the emotional weight; chrome should support it quietly.

For the landing and admin panel, extend that same restraint into a lighter, editorial web language: warm neutrals, deliberate typography, and emphasis through composition instead of badges, cards everywhere, or salesy UI conventions.

Anti-direction: avoid visual noise, excessive translucency, random accent bursts, and components that look inflated or toy-like.

### Design Principles
1. Let music metadata and artwork be the heroes; UI chrome should recede.
2. Prefer restrained surfaces with clear hierarchy over highly stylized containers.
3. Keep motion and color expressive but controlled, never distracting from playback.
4. Make dense information feel calm through spacing, typography, and alignment.
5. Evolve the existing visual language instead of replacing it with a new one.
6. On the web, say less: every sentence should earn its place.
